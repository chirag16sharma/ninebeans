The History of Coffee

A Beverage Born
from Legend

Long before the first espresso machine was ever dreamed of, coffee began its journey in the highlands of ancient Ethiopia — legend has it that a goat herder named Kaldi noticed his flock dancing with unusual energy after eating berries from a certain tree.

From the coffeehouses of 15th‑century Arabia to the grand salons of 18th‑century Europe, coffee has always been more than a drink. It has been a gathering place for thought, revolution, and connection.

At Nine Beans, we honor this legacy with every single cup we pour — tracing flavor back to its origin, its soil, its story.

c. 850 AD
Coffee discovered in the Ethiopian highlands. The legend of Kaldi and the dancing goats is born.
1450s
First coffeehouses — qahveh khaneh — open across the Arabian Peninsula, becoming centers of intellectual life.
1600s
Coffee reaches Europe. London's coffeehouses become known as "penny universities" — a seat of ideas for the price of a cup.
1884
The first espresso machine is patented in Turin, Italy — forever transforming how the world brews.
2000s–Today
Third‑wave coffee elevates beans as fine ingredients — sourced, roasted, and brewed with artisanal precision. Nine Beans is born from this movement.

"Coffee is a language in itself — a ritual, a moment of pause, a reason to sit with someone and truly be present."

— Nine Beans Philosophy

About Us

Nine Beans,
One Purpose

Founded in 2019 by two baristas with a shared obsession, Nine Beans was born from the belief that great coffee deserves the same reverence as fine wine. We source directly from farmers, roast in small batches weekly, and train our baristas like craftspeople.

We aren't just a café — we are a community of people who believe in slowing down, savoring, and sharing something truly exceptional with the world.

Direct Trade
We pay 40% above fair-trade pricing to every partner farm.
Small-Batch Roasting
Every lot is roasted fresh weekly in our own in-house roastery.
SCA Certified
All baristas trained to Specialty Coffee Association Gold Level.
Zero Waste
Compostable cups, repurposed grounds, and solar-powered stores.
