The History of Coffee

A Beverage Born
from Legend

Long before the first espresso machine was ever dreamed of, coffee began its journey in the highlands of ancient Ethiopia — legend has it that a goat herder named Kaldi first noticed his flock dancing with unusual energy after eating red berries from a certain tree.

From the coffeehouses of 15th century Arabia to the grand salons of 18th century Europe, coffee has always been more than a drink — it has been a gathering place for thought, revolution, and connection.

At Nine Beans, we honor this history with every single cup we pour.

850 AD
Coffee discovered in the Ethiopian highlands. Legend of Kaldi and the dancing goats begins.
1450s
First coffeehouses — qahveh khaneh — open in the Arabian Peninsula, becoming centers of intellectual exchange.
1600s
Coffee reaches Europe. The first coffeehouse opens in Venice, then London's "penny universities" follow.
1884
The first espresso machine is patented in Turin, Italy, forever changing how the world brews.
Today
Third-wave coffee elevates beans as fine ingredients — sourced, roasted, and brewed with artisanal care.

"Coffee is a language in itself — a ritual, a moment of pause, a reason to sit with someone and truly be present."

— Nine Beans Philosophy

About Us

Nine Beans,
One Purpose

Founded in 2019 by two baristas with a shared obsession, Nine Beans was born from the belief that great coffee deserves the same reverence as fine wine. We source directly from farmers, roast in small batches, and train our baristas like craftspeople.

We aren't just a café — we are a community of people who believe in slowing down, savoring, and sharing something truly exceptional.

Direct Trade
We pay 40% above fair trade pricing to our partner farms.
Small-Batch Roasting
Every lot is roasted fresh weekly in our own roastery.
Certified Baristas
All staff trained to SCA Gold Level certification.
Zero Waste
Compostable cups, repurposed grounds, solar-powered stores.
